# Source and additions

The retained implementation comes from [dgalor/Event2Audio](https://github.com/dgalor/Event2Audio/tree/30195c6155b730511dd90172bf420a21dca8ea25), snapshot `30195c6155b730511dd90172bf420a21dca8ea25`. The source is associated with event-based optical vibration sensing; the named authors are Mingxuan Cai, Dekel Galor, Amit Kohli, Jacob Yates and Laura Waller.

EventizedAudio adds the unified command, batch failure handling, reproducible dependency pins, synthetic parity checks and documentation, maintained by nazeeh111. These additions do not establish new authorship of the research method or independently reproduce the source-reported scientific results.

The original 2025 Mingxuan Cai & Dekel Galor MIT notice is restored in [LICENSE](LICENSE). New additions use [LICENSE-branding](LICENSE-branding). The reconstruction files and baseline fixtures are preserved. The external research data and Metavision SDK retain their own terms; neither is bundled or newly verified here.
